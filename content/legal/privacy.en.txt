Our processing of personal data is based on legal grounds under KVKK, including:
• Consent: Where required, we obtain your explicit consent for specific data processing activities.

Under KVKK, you have the following rights concerning your personal data:

• Right to Rectify: To request corrections if your data is incomplete or inaccurate.
• Right to Erasure: To request deletion or anonymization of your data under certain conditions.
• Right to Restrict Processing: To limit how we use your data.
• Right to Object: To object to specific types of data processing.
